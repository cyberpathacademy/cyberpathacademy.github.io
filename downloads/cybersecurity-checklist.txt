CyberPath Academy - Beginner Cybersecurity Checklist
=====================================================

Passwords
[ ] Every account has its own password
[ ] My passwords are at least 12 characters long
[ ] I use a password manager
[ ] Two-factor authentication is turned on for email and social media

Email and Messages
[ ] I check the sender's address before clicking a link
[ ] I never share passwords or codes by email or chat
[ ] I report suspicious messages instead of replying to them

Devices
[ ] My phone and computer have automatic updates turned on
[ ] My screen locks automatically with a PIN or password
[ ] Antivirus protection is installed and working
[ ] I back up my important files regularly

Browsing and Wi-Fi
[ ] I look for https and the padlock before entering personal information
[ ] I download apps and files only from official sources
[ ] I avoid banking or shopping on public Wi-Fi
[ ] My home Wi-Fi has a strong password

Review this checklist once a month.
