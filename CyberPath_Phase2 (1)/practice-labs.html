Practice Labs

Safe activities to practise what you learn. Never type your real passwords here.

Lab 1: Password Strength Checker

Objective: see how length, capital letters and numbers make a password stronger.

Lab 2: Spot the Phishing Email

Objective: find the warning signs in a fake email.

From: security@my-bank-support.example
Subject: URGENT! Your account will be closed
Message: Dear customer, click here within 24 hours and enter your password to keep your account.

How many warning signs can you find? Then click the button.

  • Strange sender address that does not match the real bank.
  • Urgent, scary subject to make you panic.
  • Generic greeting: "Dear customer" instead of your name.
  • Asks for your password through a link.
  • A deadline of 24 hours to pressure you.

Lab 3: Website Link Checker

Objective: learn to check if a web address uses a secure connection.
