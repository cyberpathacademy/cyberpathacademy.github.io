Why Phishing Is Still a Common Online Scam

Phishing works because it plays with emotions such as fear and curiosity.

Attackers send messages that look like they come from banks, delivery companies or social media. The goal is to make you click a link and share a password or card number. Always stop, check the sender and open the official website yourself.

Guide

Five Signs Your Device May Have Malware

Is your device slow or showing strange pop-ups? It may be a warning sign.

Common signs are a sudden slowdown, programs you did not install, many pop-up ads, a battery that drains quickly and settings that change by themselves. If you notice them, run a security scan and update your device.

Tips

Why Software Updates Matter

Updates are not only about new features, they also fix security holes.

When a weakness is found in an app or system, the company releases an update to fix it. If you delay updates, attackers can use the old weakness. Turn on automatic updates to stay protected without thinking about it.

Tips

Staying Safe on Public Wi-Fi

Free Wi-Fi is useful, but other people on the same network may see your activity.

Avoid logging in to banking or shopping websites on public Wi-Fi. Prefer websites that start with https, turn off automatic connection to open networks and use a VPN if you need to work on a public network.
