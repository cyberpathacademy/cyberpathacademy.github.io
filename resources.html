Cybersecurity Terms

  • Phishing: A trick that uses fake emails or websites to steal your information.
  • Malware: Harmful software such as viruses, spyware and ransomware.
  • Firewall: A security tool that blocks unwanted connections to your device.
  • Encryption: Turning data into a secret code so only the right person can read it.
  • Two-Factor Authentication (2FA): A second step, such as a code on your phone, to prove it is really you.
  • VPN: A service that protects your internet connection, useful on public Wi-Fi.
  • Ransomware: Malware that locks your files and asks for money to open them.
  • Backup: A copy of your files stored safely in another place.
