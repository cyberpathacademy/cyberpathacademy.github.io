Step-by-Step Tutorials

Short guides you can follow in a few minutes.

How to Create Strong Passwords

A strong password is your first line of defence.

  1. Use at least 12 characters.
  2. Mix upper and lower case letters, numbers and symbols.
  3. Never use the same password on two accounts.
  4. Use a password manager and turn on two-factor authentication.

How to Spot a Phishing Email

Phishing tricks you into giving away private information.

  1. Check the sender's email address carefully.
  2. Move your mouse over a link to see where it really goes.
  3. Be careful with urgent or threatening messages.
  4. Never type your password after clicking an email link. Open the website yourself.

How to Secure Your Device

Small steps keep your phone and computer safe.

  1. Turn on automatic software updates.
  2. Use a screen lock with a PIN or password.
  3. Install trusted antivirus software.
  4. Back up your important files regularly.

How to Browse Safely

Safe habits protect you every time you go online.

  1. Look for https and the padlock before entering information.
  2. Download apps and files only from official sources.
  3. Avoid banking or shopping on public Wi-Fi.
  4. Log out when you use a shared computer.
